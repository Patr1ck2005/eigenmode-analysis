# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Integrated the frozen `stable-v1.0.0` raw-observation band-grouping policy
  under the opt-in `eigenmode_analysis.band_grouping` API.
- Added typed original-grid/candidate inputs, per-observation band IDs,
  read-only raw grids, ordinary triangle connectivity and separate diagnostics.
- Kept all original observations, multiplicities and genuine missing support;
  no fitted vertices, renderer, consumer or benchmark dependencies enter runtime.
- Preserved the existing public source-line path/graph and numerical APIs.
  This is not a replacement for extra unpublished modules in historical local
  wheels, or an automatic upgrade of running consumer environments.
- Kept the scientific scope of the frozen research release: conditional
  support and unresolved cases do not become claims of unique physical identity.

## 0.2.10

- Added schema-neutral fitting, field-regime classification, and propagation
  primitives.
- Preserved explicit array contracts and consumer-independent imports.

Earlier internal development history is intentionally not part of the public
release branch.
